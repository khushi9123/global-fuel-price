02 / HOW TO READ IT

From raw data
to a useful view.

A simple path through the project helps keep the interpretation grounded. The dashboard is for exploration; the story offers a guided route through selected comparisons.

STEP 01

Check the context

Confirm the selected period, country coverage, fuel measure, units and any filters.

STEP 02

Compare fairly

Use consistent measures and remember that currencies, taxes and national policies differ.

STEP 03

Trace the pattern

Read time-series and ranking views as evidence of patterns, not automatic proof of causes.

STEP 04

Check the caveats

Look for incomplete periods, missing observations, aggregation choices and source limitations.

05 / INTERPRET WITH CARE

Good comparisons
need good context.

Country-level price differences can be useful signals, but the same number can mean different things depending on how it was measured. Keep these checks in mind while exploring the dashboard.

Back to the top ↑
↔

Check the unit and currency

Confirm that the values use a comparable unit and currency before interpreting the gap.

◷

Check the time window

A full-year comparison and a partial-year snapshot may not tell the same story.

≋

Separate tax measures

A tax amount, tax rate and tax share are distinct measures and should be labelled separately.

⌁

Distinguish pattern from cause

Observed association does not prove that a single factor caused a price difference.
